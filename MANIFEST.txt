BUG #1 — the literal \u25b6 / \u2014 text
──────────────────────────────
Real bug, not your setup. When I write special characters as raw JSX text
(not inside an actual JS string), the browser doesn't interpret backslash-u
escapes — it prints the six characters literally. That's what you were
seeing on the Present buttons, the "All decks" link, the arrows in Checkout
→, the workflow breadcrumbs, and a few spots in the investor deck's visuals
too (→ · — ✓ × etc.). Fixed by replacing every instance with the actual
character, in:
  src/pages/PitchDeckPage.jsx
  src/pages/PitchLandingPage.jsx
  src/components/pitch/visuals/demoVisuals.jsx
  src/components/pitch/visuals/investorVisuals.jsx

──────────────────────────────
